Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

DyGarn Technical Solutions ("DyGarn", "we", "us") is a technical and data consultancy based in Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada. This policy explains what information we collect through our website at dygarn.com, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to the DyGarn marketing website. Our SaaS product, Padma, has its own Privacy Policy.

Information we collect

Information you give us

When you contact us through a form, email, or by booking a call, we collect the details you provide, such as your name, email address, company, and the contents of your message. We use this to respond to you and to provide the services you request.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we collect standard analytics data when you visit, including pages viewed, approximate location derived from your IP address, device and browser type, and referring source. We use Google Analytics (GA4) for this. We do not use this data to identify you personally.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide requested services;
  • To operate, maintain, and improve our website;
  • To understand how visitors use the site, in aggregate;
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that help us run the site and our business, and only as needed to perform their function.

Service providers

We rely on a small number of trusted third parties, including Google Analytics (website analytics), Formspree (contact form delivery), and Calendly (call scheduling). Each processes data only to provide its service to us and under its own privacy terms.

Cookies

We use essential cookies to make the site work and analytics cookies to understand usage. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some features may not work as intended if you do.

Data retention

We keep the information you send us for as long as needed to respond, deliver services, and meet legal or accounting requirements, after which we delete or anonymize it.

Your rights

Under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA) and comparable laws, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you, and you may withdraw consent. To make a request, email hello@dygarn.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and to limit access to it.

Children

Our website and services are intended for businesses and are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Email hello@dygarn.com or write to DyGarn Technical Solutions, Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada.
